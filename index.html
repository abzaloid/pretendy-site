Pretendy

Pretend play for children on iPhone – run the café, play school, scoop ice cream, care for poorly pets and more, with Nuno the otter.

Privacy Policy Support & FAQ

Six places to play

  • Free: the Café and School.
  • With Pretendy Plus: the Ice cream parlour, Restaurant, Vet and Hair salon. Each Plus place has one free job to try.

Finished jobs earn stars, and a full star jar means a new sticker. There's one design for every child, with no ages or levels to choose. Playing together on two nearby iPhones is coming in a later update.

For parents, in short

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
  • Pretendy collects no personal data. Game progress stays on your device.
  • No camera, no microphone and no chat.
  • Purchases, links and settings for grown-ups sit behind a parental gate.

Pretendy is made by an independent developer based in the UK.

Playing together

How does playing together work?

Two or more iPhones or iPads that are near each other can join the same game. They connect directly using Apple's local Wi-Fi and Bluetooth technology (MultipeerConnectivity). No internet connection or account is needed, and only game moves are shared between the devices.

The devices can't find each other. What can I try?

  • Keep the devices close together, with Pretendy open on each one.
  • Make sure Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are switched on in Control Centre or Settings on every device (you don't need to be connected to a Wi-Fi network).
  • If iOS asked for permission to find devices on your local network and it was declined, turn it on in Settings › Pretendy.
  • Close and reopen the app on each device, then try again.

Pretendy Plus and restoring purchases

What is Pretendy Plus?

Pretendy Plus is an optional upgrade bought through Apple's In-App Purchase. It is available as:

UK prices. Prices in other countries are set by the App Store and shown before you buy.
OptionPrice
Lifetime (one-off)£29.99
Monthly subscription£2.99 per month
Annual subscription£14.99 per year, with a 1-week free trial

Purchases are behind a parental gate, so a grown-up needs to complete them. Apple handles the payment and we never see your payment details. Pretendy Plus supports Family Sharing, so members of your Apple Family Sharing group can use it too. Use of the app is covered by Apple's Standard End User Licence Agreement.

How do I restore my purchase on a new or reset device?

  1. Make sure the device is signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account used to buy Pretendy Plus (or one in the same Family Sharing group).
  2. Open Pretendy and go to the Pretendy Plus screen (you will need to pass the parental gate).
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.

There is no Pretendy account to sign in to – your purchase is linked to your Apple Account.

Cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To cancel or change one on your iPhone or iPad:

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Pretendy, then Cancel Subscription.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. If you are on the free trial and don't want to be charged, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. After cancelling, Plus stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com – refunds are handled by Apple.

The parental gate

What is the parental gate?

It is a simple check designed for grown-ups that appears before anything meant for adults, such as purchases. It helps stop children buying things by accident. If you can't get past it, please contact us.

Sound and voice

I can't hear anything.

  • Turn up the volume using the buttons on the side of the device.
  • Check that the device isn't connected to Bluetooth headphones or a speaker you didn't expect.
  • Check the sound and voice options in Pretendy's settings.

Where does the voice come from?

Pretendy talks using Apple's built-in text-to-speech on your device, or recordings included in the app. It does not record your child's voice.

Privacy

Pretendy collects no personal data: no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

Notifications: the app does not send notifications to children. Any notifications for parents are opt-in only and can be turned off in Settings › Notifications.

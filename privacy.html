Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

The short version

Pretendy collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. The app has no servers of its own, and nothing about your child is sent to us.

Who we are

Pretendy is a pretend-play app for children (café, shops, School and more) for iPhone and iPad, listed in the App Store Kids Category. It is made by an individual developer based in the United Kingdom (App Store developer account: abzaloid). The app's bundle ID is cool.pretend.games. In this policy “we” and “us” means that developer.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data from children or adults. We do not use cookies, advertising IDs, analytics, crash-reporting SDKs or any other tracking.

What stays on your device

To make the game work, Pretendy saves a few things only on your device:

  • game progress, stars and stickers;
  • the age your child chose in the app (an age only – never a birth date);
  • app settings;
  • a list of recently seen questions, so the game doesn't repeat itself too often.

This information is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it. Like any app data, it may be included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups, which you control.

Playing together on nearby devices

The “play together” feature connects nearby iPhones and iPads directly using Apple's MultipeerConnectivity, over local Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. Only game moves are shared between the connected devices. Each installation of the app has a random device ID that is used only to tell devices apart during a local game. Nothing goes over the internet and nothing is sent to us. iOS may ask your permission before the app can find nearby devices.

Camera

Some games include a pretend “play scanner” that uses the camera through Apple's VisionKit. The camera is used live, on the device, in the game. Images are not saved and are not shared. iOS will ask your permission before the camera is used, and you can change this at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Speech and sound

Pretendy's voice is produced on the device by Apple's built-in text-to-speech or by recordings included in the app. The app does not record your child's voice.

Purchases

Pretendy Plus is an optional upgrade sold through Apple's In-App Purchase, behind a parental gate. Apple handles the payment. We never receive your name, card or payment details. The app checks with Apple, on the device, whether Pretendy Plus is active; this is not sent to us. Apple's handling of your payment is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

The app does not send notifications to children. Any notifications for parents are opt-in only: they are off unless a parent chooses to turn them on, and they can be turned off at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Children's privacy

Pretendy is designed for children. We have built it to be consistent with the UK GDPR, the ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code (the Children's Code), the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's App Store rules for the Kids Category. Because we collect no personal information from children, there is nothing for us to share, sell or use for advertising, and there are no ads.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over your personal data, including the right to ask what is held about you and to have it deleted. As we hold no personal data about you or your child, there is nothing for us to give or delete, but you are welcome to contact us with any question. You can delete everything the app stores at any time by deleting the app. If you have a concern, you can also contact the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Pretendy handles information, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top. We will not start collecting personal data from children without updating this policy first.

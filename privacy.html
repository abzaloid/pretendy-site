Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

The short version

Pretendy collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. Pretendy has no servers, and nothing about your child is ever sent to us. The little the app saves stays on your device.

Who we are

Pretendy (“the app”) is a pretend-play app for children, for iPhone, in the App Store Kids Category. It is made and published as Pretendy by an independent developer based in the United Kingdom (App Store developer account: abzaloid). The app's bundle ID is cool.pretend.games. You can contact us at support.pretendy@gmail.com. “We”, “us” and “our” in this policy mean Pretendy, that developer. For UK data protection law we would be the controller of any personal data we held, but as explained below we don't hold any.

What Pretendy is

Your child plays in six places with Nuno the otter as their guide: the Café, School, the Ice cream parlour, the Restaurant, the Vet and the Hair salon. The Café and School are free. Pretendy Plus, an optional in-app purchase, unlocks the other four. Finished jobs earn stars and stickers. Play together, for two nearby devices, is coming in a later update; this version doesn't include it.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't collect, receive, store, sell or share personal data from children or adults. We don't use cookies, advertising identifiers (IDFA), analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, fingerprinting or any other tracking. The app doesn't use App Tracking Transparency because it doesn't track.

Pretendy does not:

  • use the camera, photos, microphone, location, contacts or health data;
  • record your child's voice (Nuno's voice and the other characters' voices are recordings that ship with the app);
  • let children chat, type messages to each other or post anything publicly;
  • show advertising of any kind;
  • send push notifications.

What stays on your device

So the game works, Pretendy saves a few things only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • Player profiles, if a grown-up adds them: an optional first name or nickname and a picture (avatar) chosen from the app's set. There's no age or date of birth.
  • Game progress: stars, stickers, which places have been played, and whether a place's free taster job has been used.
  • Settings, such as sound and voice, new visitors during free play, and the first-time tips.
  • Today's pretend orders, which are cleared automatically a couple of hours after they're finished.
  • A random identifier for this installation of the app, kept for Play together (see below). In this version it never leaves the device. It isn't linked to you, your Apple Account or your device's hardware.

None of this is sent to us. A grown-up can remove a player (with their stars and stickers) or clear today's orders in the app's Grown-ups area. Deleting the app deletes everything it saved. Like all app data, it may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, which you control.

The parental gate

Before the shop, links that leave the app and settings, Pretendy asks a grown-up to pass a parental gate. The year typed in to pass it is checked once on the device and never stored or sent anywhere.

Playing together on nearby devices (coming in a later update)

This version of Pretendy doesn't include Play together. It never searches for or connects to other devices, and it doesn't ask for permission to use your local network. When Play together arrives in a later update, it will work as described below, and we'll update this policy first.

Play together lets two devices near each other join the same game, for example one child serving in the café while the other visits as the customer. It uses Apple's MultipeerConnectivity framework, which connects devices directly over Bluetooth or local Wi-Fi.

  • It's off until you turn it on. A grown-up starts Play together from behind the parental gate the first time. iOS then asks for permission to find devices on your local network. You can change this at any time in Settings › Pretendy.
  • No internet and no servers. The devices talk only to each other. Nothing passes through us or anyone else.
  • Encrypted. Connections between the devices are encrypted (the app requires encryption).
  • What nearby devices can see while it's searching: the random identifier for this installation, a three-animal play code, and the avatar picture of the current player. Never a name.
  • What connected devices share: the game itself (the pretend orders, which place and role each device is in, pretend phone calls with no audio), the app's version number, and the device's generic model name as iOS provides it (for example “iPhone”), not the name you've given your device.
  • What they never share: profile names, stars, stickers, settings, or any free text or chat.
  • Searching stops by itself after about a minute if no one is found. After a recent connection, the app may quietly try to reconnect for up to 30 seconds.

Purchases

Pretendy Plus (monthly or annual subscription, or a one-off Lifetime purchase) is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase, behind the parental gate. Apple handles the payment, and we never receive your name, email, card or payment details. The app asks Apple, on the device, whether Pretendy Plus is active. That answer isn't sent to us. Family Sharing and “Restore purchases” are handled by Apple in the same way. Apple's handling of payments is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Ratings

The app may show Apple's standard “rate this app” prompt to a grown-up inside the Grown-ups area, never to a child. Any rating or review you give goes to Apple, not to us.

If you contact us

The Grown-ups area has a “Tell us what your child thinks” link. It opens a draft email in your own email app, and nothing is sent unless you choose to send it. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use these only to reply to you, and we delete them when they're no longer needed. Please don't include personal details about your child.

Links

Links to this policy, our support page and Apple's Terms of Use sit behind the parental gate and open outside the app.

Children's privacy

Pretendy is made for children, and we built it to collect nothing about them. We aim to meet:

  • UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018 and the ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code (the Children's Code). We collect no personal data. Settings are private by default. This version has no Play together, and when it arrives it will be off until a grown-up turns it on. There's no profiling, no geolocation and no tracking. Nothing is shared with third parties. The app uses no nudge techniques: no streaks, no timers, no random or paid rewards and no pressure to buy. Purchases and settings are for grown-ups only.
  • The US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We don't collect personal information from children, including persistent identifiers. The random installation identifier never leaves the device in this version (in a later version with Play together, only to a nearby device in a local game), and it is never sent to us or used to track or advertise. Because we collect nothing, there is nothing for us to share, sell, use for advertising or delete.
  • Apple's App Store rules for the Kids Category. No third-party analytics or advertising, and a parental gate before purchases, links and settings.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (and similar laws elsewhere) you have rights over your personal data, including the right to ask what is held and to have it deleted. We hold no personal data about you or your child through the app, so in practice there is nothing to give you or delete. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to delete that correspondence. You can delete everything the app stores by deleting the app. If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If Pretendy's handling of information changes, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top before the change reaches the app. We won't start collecting personal data from children without updating this policy first and getting any consent the law requires.
